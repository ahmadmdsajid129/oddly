# Situations Lane Editorial & Safety Guidelines (Rule L7 & Feature F36)

> **Document Status:** Authoritative Content Policy & Editorial Safety Manual  
> **Target Milestone:** Stage L5 (Phase 10P)  
> **Governance:** `rules.md` Rule L7 (Situations Safety & Kindness), Section 9.1 (Forbidden Topics), and ADR 045

---

## 1. Core Mission & Philosophical Invariants

The **Situations** lane presents players with relatable, everyday social dilemmas (*"What would you do?"*), revealing a descriptive behavioral style label that reflects how they navigate unexpected moments.

### The Rule L7 Safety Mandates (Non-Negotiable)
1. **Zero Shaming, Zero Ranking:** We never evaluate human worth, attractiveness, or social hierarchy. There are no "good" or "bad" reaction styles.
2. **Strictly Forbidden Archetypes:** Absolutely NO toxic internet tropes or evaluative labels (no *"sigma"*, *"simp"*, *"desperate"*, *"loser"*, *"alpha"*, *"beta"*, *"cringe"*).
3. **Gender-Neutral by Design:** Every scene must be written so it applies equally regardless of the gender or sexual orientation of the player or others in the scene.
4. **Zero Pursuit or Romantic Pressure:** No scenarios involving stalking, persistent unwanted advances, dating coercion, or non-consensual dynamics. All scenarios are framed around platonic, workplace, campus, or everyday social etiquette, maintaining full compliance with Section 9.1 Item 4.
5. **18+ Contextual Relevance:** Dilemmas reflect adult life (workplace awkwardness, shared apartment living, friend group dynamics, family gatherings).
6. **No Percentage Scores on Style:** Style cards present descriptive personality labels only (e.g. *"The Playful Spark"*). Never display numbers like *"85% Bold"* or *"Attractiveness: 90%"*.

## 2. Mandatory 8-Point Pre-Publication Safety Checklist

Every draft scenario for the Situations lane must pass all 8 checks before approval:

- [ ] **1. Gender Neutrality:** Scene uses neutral pronouns ("someone", "a colleague", "a friend") and makes no gendered assumptions.
- [ ] **2. No Romantic Coercion:** Scene involves zero unwanted pursuit, dating pressure, or physical intimacy dilemmas.
- [ ] **3. Platonically Framed:** Complies with Section 9.1 Item 4 (Sexuality & Romance exclusion); focuses on general social reaction style.
- [ ] **4. Kindness Invariant:** None of the options or reaction cards shame the player or encourage cruelty toward others.
- [ ] **5. Balanced Style Spread:** The 3 to 5 reaction options map cleanly to distinct behavioral styles without a single "obviously correct" answer.
- [ ] **6. No Forbidden Topics:** Free from all Section 9.1 forbidden categories (religion, caste, politics, mental health diagnoses, celebrities, brands).
- [ ] **7. Clear Physical Action:** Each option describes a concrete, relatable reaction rather than abstract psychological self-analysis.
- [ ] **8. Two-Person Sign-Off:** Reviewed and approved by two independent editors, with final confirmation by the Founder.

## 4. 10 Approved Scenario Examples (Good Scenarios)

These scenarios strictly satisfy Rule L7 and Section 9.1:

1. **The Unexpected Conversation:** *"Someone you've noticed often at campus or work suddenly walks up and says hello. How do you react?"*
   - Options: Smile warmly and say hi / Freeze for a second, then laugh / Ask a quick question to see what they need / Nod politely and wait for them to speak first.
2. **The Accidental Reply-All:** *"A teammate accidentally hits 'Reply All' with an embarrassing typo in front of the whole department. What do you do?"*
   - Options: Send a quick meme in the group chat to normalize it / Pretend you never saw it / Reply privately with a reassuring message / Change the subject to the next agenda item.
3. **The Shared Elevator Silence:** *"You're stuck in an elevator for 30 seconds with an acquaintance you barely know. What's your move?"*
   - Options: Pull out your phone and inspect the lock screen / Make a comment about the weather or elevator speed / Smile, say hi, and embrace the quiet / Ask them how their day is going.
4. **The Split Bill Dilemma:** *"At a group dinner, one person ordered expensive drinks while everyone else had simple snacks, but they suggest splitting equally. What do you do?"*
   - Options: Pay the split without saying a word to avoid awkwardness / Lightly joke: 'Wait, let's tally the smoothies!' / Quietly calculate your exact share and transfer that / Suggest splitting by what was ordered.
5. **The Spilled Chai:** *"You accidentally knock over a paper cup of chai on a colleague's desk, barely missing their notebook. How do you react?"*
   - Options: Instantly grab tissues and apologize profusely / Calmly evaluate the spill, move the notebook, then clean / Laugh at your own clumsiness and offer to buy them a fresh chai / Apologize sincerely and clean the entire desk.
6. **The Unfinished Handshake:** *"You go in for a handshake, but the other person goes in for a fist bump, creating an awkward hand mash. What do you do?"*
   - Options: Laugh out loud and reset the handshake / Turn it into a goofy explosion hand sign / Pretend nothing happened and keep talking / Smile and say: 'Let's try that one more time!'
7. **The Wrong Name:** *"Someone you've met three times confidently introduces you to a friend using the wrong name. How do you handle it?"*
   - Options: Correct them immediately with a smile / Let it slide for now and mention your real name later / Casually slip your name into the conversation / Laugh and say: 'Close! It's actually [Name].'
8. **The Surprise Praise:** *"During a routine meeting, your lead unexpectedly calls you out for doing an incredible job on a project. How do you react?"*
   - Options: Blush, look down, and smile / Immediately credit the teammates who helped / Nod confidently and say thank you / Deflect with a self-deprecating joke.
9. **The Group Chat Silence:** *"You post what you think is a hilarious photo in a close group chat, and nobody replies for four hours. What's your reaction?"*
   - Options: Delete the message quietly / Post a self-mocking follow-up meme / Completely forget about it; they're probably busy / Check if your Wi-Fi is working.
10. **The Forgotten Plan:** *"A friend texts: 'Running 20 minutes late!' when you've already arrived at the cafe. What do you do?"*
    - Options: Order your drink and enjoy 20 minutes of peaceful reading / Text back: 'Take your time, no rush at all!' / Explore the nearby shops while waiting / Joke: 'You owe me a cookie!'

## 6. Result & Share Card Presentation Rules

1. **Style Description Only:** Result cards state the archetype title and a warm 2-line description.
2. **Zero Numerical Scores:** Never display percentages, ranking tiers, or points.
3. **Twin Display:** Twin matching in Situations pairs players whose overall reaction patterns aligned, highlighting shared style labels.
4. **Export Card Integrity:** Share card exports generated via Satori preserve identical sticker borders, ink drop shadows, and non-evaluative copy.
